# 🎧 Phase 10 — Speech & Audio AI

Phase 10 focuses on one of the most human forms of AI interaction:

> understanding and generating speech and audio.

This phase connects AI to real-world human communication, enabling systems that can:
- listen
- understand
- speak
- and react in real time

---

# 🎯 Purpose of This Phase

The goal of Phase 10 is to understand and build:

- speech-to-text systems (ASR)
- real-time voice assistants
- speaker recognition systems
- text-to-speech generation models
- audio event detection systems

By the end of this phase, you will be able to build **AI systems that interact through voice and sound**.

---

# 🧠 What You Will Learn

You will develop intuition for:

- how audio signals are represented digitally
- how speech is converted into text
- how neural networks process waveforms and spectrograms
- how speaker identity can be learned
- how text is converted into natural speech
- how audio events are detected in real time

---

# ⚙️ Core Topics Covered

## 🎤 Speech-to-Text (ASR)
- automatic speech recognition
- transcription pipelines
- language modeling in speech

## 🗣️ Text-to-Speech (TTS)
- speech synthesis
- voice conditioning
- prosody and intonation modeling

## 👤 Speaker Identification
- speaker embeddings
- voice fingerprinting
- classification of speakers

## 🎧 Audio Processing
- waveforms vs spectrograms
- feature extraction (MFCCs)
- signal preprocessing

## 🔊 Audio Event Detection
- classification of sound events
- environmental audio understanding
- temporal audio modeling

---

# 🏗️ Project Roadmap (5–10 Projects)

Projects progress from basic audio processing → full real-time voice systems.

---

## 🟢 Project 1 — Speech-to-Text System

### 🎯 Goal
Convert spoken audio into written text.

### Concepts
- ASR pipelines
- Whisper-based transcription
- audio preprocessing

### Outcome
A system that converts audio files into accurate text transcripts.

---

## 🟢 Project 2 — Real-Time Voice Assistant

### 🎯 Goal
Build a voice-controlled AI system.

### Concepts
- streaming audio input
- real-time inference
- command parsing

### Outcome
An assistant that listens and responds in real time.

---

## 🟡 Project 3 — Speaker Identification System

### 🎯 Goal
Recognize who is speaking.

### Concepts
- speaker embeddings
- classification models
- voice similarity metrics

### Outcome
A system that identifies speakers from audio samples.

---

## 🟡 Project 4 — Audio Event Detection System

### 🎯 Goal
Detect sounds in the environment.

### Concepts
- environmental sound classification
- spectrogram analysis
- temporal modeling

### Outcome
A system that detects events like alarms, footsteps, or voices.

---

## 🔵 Project 5 — Voice Cloning System

### 🎯 Goal
Generate synthetic speech in a specific voice.

### Concepts
- text-to-speech models
- speaker conditioning
- audio synthesis

### Outcome
A system that can imitate a speaker’s voice style (educational use).

---

## 🔵 Project 6 — Speech Emotion Recognition

### 🎯 Goal
Understand emotional tone in speech.

### Concepts
- audio feature extraction
- classification models
- affective computing

### Outcome
A system that detects emotions like happy, sad, or angry from voice.

---

## 🔵 Project 7 — Streaming Speech Pipeline

### 🎯 Goal
Build a full real-time audio processing system.

### Concepts
- streaming inference
- buffering audio chunks
- latency optimization

### Outcome
A pipeline that transcribes and responds in real time.

---

## 🔴 Project 8 — Multi-Speaker Dialogue System

### 🎯 Goal
Handle conversations with multiple speakers.

### Concepts
- diarization (who spoke when)
- speaker tracking
- conversation segmentation

### Outcome
A system that separates and understands multi-speaker conversations.

---

## 🔴 Project 9 — Audio Understanding Agent

### 🎯 Goal
Combine speech understanding with reasoning.

### Concepts
- ASR + LLM integration
- audio summarization
- context reasoning

### Outcome
An agent that listens to audio and explains or summarizes it.

---

## 🔴 Project 10 — Full Voice AI System

### 🎯 Goal
Build a complete voice-based AI assistant.

### Concepts
- ASR + TTS + LLM integration
- real-time interaction loop
- system orchestration

### Outcome
A fully functional voice assistant that can:
- listen
- understand
- reason
- respond naturally
